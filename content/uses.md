What I use day to day. Configs live in [nixos](https://github.com/juniorsundar/nixos), [nvim](https://github.com/juniorsundar/nvim) and [dotfiles](https://github.com/juniorsundar/dotfiles).

## Desk

- **Desktop:** Intel Core i7-13700K, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090, 32 GB RAM, ASUS PRIME B760M-K
- TODO: laptop, monitors, keyboard, mouse

## Software

- **OS:** NixOS (flakes), KDE Plasma
- **Editor:** Neovim (nightly), with my own [refer.nvim](https://github.com/juniorsundar/refer.nvim) and [cling.nvim](https://github.com/juniorsundar/cling.nvim)
- **Terminal:** Ghostty, Lilex Nerd Font, Doom One theme
- **Shell:** zsh with Starship
- **Tools:** lazygit, yazi
- **Notes:** Obsidian, published as my [digital garden](garden:)

## Robotics

- **Middleware:** ROS 2, Nav2, MoveIt
- **Simulation:** Gazebo, PX4 SITL/HITL
- **Flight stack:** PX4-Autopilot on Pixhawk-class flight controllers
- **Compute:** NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX, Raspberry Pi
- **Platforms:** Holybro X500 and T-Motor M690B quadrotors, Agile Scout Mini, Unitree Go2
- **Containers:** Docker, and Nix for reproducible builds

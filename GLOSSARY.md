# juniorsundar.github.io

Personal site, blog and CV of Junior Sundar, built from one repo.

## Language

**Project**:
A piece of work with a public artifact (repo, paper, demo), recorded once and shown in any number of views. Employer work without a public artifact is not a Project; it stays in Experience.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, repo

**Project source**:
The single record of all Projects, from which every view is rendered.
_Avoid_: projects data, project list

**Experience**:
A role held at an employer, with its highlights. Work under NDA lives here, never as a Project.
_Avoid_: job, position

**Project card**:
The web view of a Project: image, name, short description, link.
_Avoid_: tile, thumbnail

**Public CV**:
The CV published on the site, with no phone or address. Shows every Project.
_Avoid_: resume

**Tailored CV**:
A CV built for one application from a chosen subset of Projects, kept within a page budget. Never published.
_Avoid_: custom CV, variant

**Full CV**:
The Public CV plus phone and address, built on demand and never committed.
_Avoid_: private CV
